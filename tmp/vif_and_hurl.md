If you're following me, you probably know that I'm currently developing a web
framework and an HTTP client in OCaml within the Robur cooperative. Their names
are: vif & hurl.

I know that the appearance of Miou has already caused debate in the OCaml
community and the development of Vif (in particular) can also cause debate when
you know what exists already.

This article mainly serves to clarify a position that I have already expressed
here and there, but it also allows me to affirm that I have never been
unfamiliar with the issue of HTTP servers in OCaml, since my very first
internship in OCaml was with the Ocsigen team.

In addition, I would especially like to share my personal (social) experience
of the OCaml community and the choices that our cooperative has been able to
make in full knowledge of what others are also trying to do.

<hr />

**disclamer**: Everything in this article corresponds to my opinion. It is not
an absolute reality and I also understand that my perspective is certainly not
the same as that of my readers. The conclusions I draw from it are not rational
either, but correspond to a material and concrete reality from which I cannot
extricate myself (I am not a philosopher).

The aim of this article is not to argue a position but to clarify. And if you
don't agree, you are of course cordially welcome to discuss it with me.
